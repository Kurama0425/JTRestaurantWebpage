## Interpretation notes

- Standard sides have no separate prices in the photo; listed +$1/+$2/+$3 amounts are supplements. No standalone side prices or entree side inclusions have been invented.
- Photo spelling “Morney” is retained in the tilapia/crab sauce descriptions. “Chocolate Chip” and “Milky Way” have no further dessert type or description printed; none is invented.
- Removed the old Mahi-Mahi, Ahi Tuna, and Chocolate Pecan offerings, which do not appear in these photos. Fish of the Day replaces the old named daily fish options. Split combined salads, desserts, and add-ons into the actual listed choices.
- Added beverages, dressings, the printed 20% gratuity for parties of six or more, and the $5 shared plate charge.
- The source's dated shortage/60–80% price-increase apology is not an item, price, or description and is not repeated on the refreshed website.
- Only punctuation, consistent money formatting, and category context such as “(kids)”/“(add-on)” are normalized. Old ingredient claims, quantities, and preparation details absent from the photos have been removed.
