Our Story

John + Travis. A place on Main.

J+T's on the Main opened in Chatham, Virginia, in August 2020, bringing John and Travis's love of good food to Main Street.

Our story has roots in JT's at the Lavalette in Gretna, where John Turner cooked before bringing the restaurant to Chatham. Here, beside the Pittsylvania County courthouse, steaks, seafood, and time around the table remain at the heart of the experience.

The J and T stand for John and Travis. Travis carries the restaurant forward, and the name stays as a tribute to the two people behind it.

In loving memory

Remembering John Turner

Chef. Guitarist. Singer. Part of our story, always.

We remember John through the food he loved to cook and the music he loved to share. A chef who also played guitar and sang, he brought both passions to his life.

Before J+T's on the Main, John ran JT's at the Lavalette in Gretna. He later brought the restaurant to Chatham, where J+T's opened in August 2020. With the restaurant now in Travis's care, John's place in its story remains.

Our name still stands for John and Travis. Keeping it is one way we honor John as we welcome guests to the table.

Draft for Travis's review. John is remembered as having spent more than 40 years as a chef and over a decade at the Lavalette. Those time spans and the exact ownership and handover chronology still need Travis's confirmation.
