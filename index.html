Welcome to J+T's on the Main

Serving up expertly cooked Steaks and Seafood for over a decade!

Our Menu Make A Reservation

J&T's on the Main has joined Chatham as one of the few restaurants in the area offering something different from all the rest.

We strive to bring you the best hand-cut grilled steaks and fresh seafood all from the heart of Chatham located next to the Pittsylvania county courthouse on main street.

    First Estabilished in 2011 as J.T.'s at the Lavalette, Chef/Owner John Turner and his son Co-owner/Sous Chef Travis Stinnett have been grilling up thick-cut steaks and fresh seafood at the original location in Gretna, VA.
    In late 2020 they moved to their new and current location on Main street Chatham and ever since have been pushing the same quality of food since, only the best!
